$$S_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+....a_{n}\text{ and } a_{1},a_{n}\text{-členi aritmetičnega zaporedja.}$$
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{n}=(a_{1}+a_{n})*2*n \\
\frac{(a_{1}+a_{n})}{2}*n\\
\frac{n}{2}(2a_{1}+(n-1)d)
\end{aligned}$$
